## Decision rule

Pass when every fixture is within tolerance. Any identity or invariant failure kills the
instrument until fixed; nothing downstream runs on it (gate G2a). Tolerances are frozen here and
are never loosened after a result is seen. Code may be fixed and E0 re-run any number of times;
every run is recorded.

- Candidate set: not applicable. Maximum runs: unlimited, all recorded.
- Compute: local CPU, under one minute. GPU-hours: 0 (AUTHORIZATION.md section 4).
- Dependencies: none.
- Result that kills the claim: any identity or invariant failure.
