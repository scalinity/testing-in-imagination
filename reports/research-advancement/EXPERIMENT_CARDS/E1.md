# E1: the exact synthetic study (parts A to D)

Study TII-CEE-1. Frozen November 13, 2026, before the first run. Specification sections 5, 6, 8.1,
9.2 to 9.5, 13, 15. Kind: new intervention; evaluation provenance: exact (twin). CPU only.

## Environment

- The synthetic twin of Edwin Salcedo's study (PR #1, commit df467ea), reached through a
  byte-exact vendored snapshot and the adapters in tii-cee/tiicee/twin, with REVIEW-002, -006 and
  -011 repaired there. Every manifest binds the snapshot digest.
- Full twin: the 12 tests of the study. Reduced twin: physical examination, CT, ultrasound,
  complete blood count, liver function panel, urinalysis (section 8.1).
- Variants used for method choices: base, no phenotype correlation, stronger correlation,
  duplicated panel. The informative-missingness variant is held out: no part of E1 uses it, and it
  appears first in M5.
- Seeds: twenty environment seeds, 0 to 19. Each seed has separate streams for generation,
  behavior, fitting, corruption and Monte Carlo (section 15). Per seed: 5,000 simulated training
  patients (fitting stream) for W1, W2, W2-cc and the held-out budget; 2,000 evaluation patients
  (generation stream) for the full-twin decision points.
- Utility: 1[correct] minus lambda times normalized charged cost; unavailable results are free and
  consume a step (REVIEW-006). Primary lambda 1; frontier at lambda 0, 0.5, 1, 2.

## Where exactness comes from

- Reduced twin: every multi-step quantity (expected log loss and accuracy at each horizon 1 to 6,
  episode utility, the terms of 5.12 (iii), the values V of part A) by exact enumeration over the
  latent case and every outcome path.
- Full twin: decision-point quantities (drift, responsiveness, KL_inc, tau_B, tau_ratio, the two
  model-free probes, identity terms, one-step regret) exactly over the outcome distribution at
  each decision point, along trajectories of the 2,000 evaluation patients per seed, horizon 8.
  Episode log loss at horizon 8 on the full twin is a Monte Carlo cross-check against the sandbox
  and is labeled Monte Carlo.

## Components (section 6.1 contract, shared by every part unless a part says otherwise)

| Component | Declaration |
|---|---|
| World model | W0 (truth), W1 (class-conditional independent counts), W2 (history-conditioned latent phenotype, evidence identity), W2-cc (its class-conditional form, source of B-model), controlled errors (part C); exact enumeration, no K |
| Reader | R0 (Bayes) and the R4 family: independence, entrenching drift (delta 0.4 to the current argmax), fixed-class drift (delta 0.4 to one fixed class), over-reactive (likelihood power 2), under-reactive (power 0.5), and the mixtures entrenching + over-reactive and fixed-class + under-reactive. Input: the history only |
| Coherence layer | None, CEE-Read stateless, CEE-Read stateful; floor 1e-6; IPF tolerance 1e-8, 500 iterations, raw reader on non-convergence |
| Scale layer | None, CEE-Scale shrink-only [1, 16], two-sided [1/8, 16]; budget source declared per row |
| Gate | None, or CEE-Gate with bounds from excluded simulated folds |
| Acquisition | S_rand: a random order drawn once per seed from the behavior stream. S_ig: greedy exact information gain under W0, deterministic. Part A adds S_raw and CEE-Select |
| Commit | Step limit; confidence threshold 0.8 (sensitivity 0.7 and 0.9); CEE-Commit; Bayes-optimal stopping (reference) |
| Availability | Population availability of the twin, as the study generates it |
| Information access | Ordinary: the history. Privileged and declared: W0 and R0 references only |
| Policy identity | Not applicable (no trained policy) |
| Pairing | Every arm sees identical latent cases by fingerprint; the unit is the patient within a seed; intervals are paired per-seed differences over the twenty seeds |

## Part A: exact attribution of regret (section 9.2)

- Agents: for each R4 reader R, the raw agent (S_raw: greedy on the reader's entropy-change
  surrogate under W2 minus lambda times expected charged cost; R raw; threshold commit 0.8) and the
  repaired agent (CEE-Select under W2; R with the CEE layer chosen in parts B and D; CEE-Commit).
- References: S*, the Bayes-optimal selection; R*, R0; C*, the Bayes-optimal stopping rule; V*, the
  Bayes-optimal value under the same observation access and step budget, by dynamic programming
  over histories. References never see unrevealed outcomes.
- Attribution: swap one component at a time to its reference in the declared order selection,
  reading, commitment; the three increments sum exactly to V* − V(S, R, C). All six orders are
  reported as a sensitivity.
- Decision rule: descriptive. The claim is killed if the spread of a component's share across the
  six orders exceeds that share's value in the declared order.

## Part B: audit, projection and gate with simulated readers (section 9.3)

- Grid: every reader (R0 and R4) × world model (W0, W1, W2, W2-cc) × variant × seed.
- Measured exactly: drift, responsiveness, KL_inc, tau_B (two-sided diagnostic, exact budget),
  tau_ratio, both model-free probes, the identity terms, expected log loss raw and corrected on
  both tracks, accuracy, one-step reader-relative regret under raw and coherent scores, episode
  utility under threshold commit and CEE-Commit. How well tau_ratio tracks tau_B: Spearman
  correlation and mean absolute log ratio.
- Track rule (section 5.9): the track used on charts is the one with lower exact expected log
  loss at the final horizon, averaged over the two drift families under W2. A tie, meaning the
  paired 95% interval of the difference over the twenty seeds includes 0, goes to stateless.
- Decision rules (section 13): repair present if both drift families improve with the paired 95%
  interval excluding 0 under W0 and under W2; no-harm if R0 with W0 changes by at most 1e-9;
  negative controls confirmed if both scale families (over- and under-reactive) worsen under
  projection alone; gate valid if allowed applications have positive mean gain for every family.
- Result that kills the claim: drift families do not improve under W0.

## Part C: reader-weighted error against likelihood (section 9.4)

- Controlled errors of the world model, built from W0: mirror corruption at strengths 0.1, 0.2,
  0.4; temperature miscalibration at 0.5 and 2; and the matched pair, two perturbations with equal
  exact KL from the truth within 0.005 nats, one in outcome directions the reader ignores (flat
  log Z_o) and one in the direction the reader responds to most.
- Per history: one-step regret of CEE-Select (true net value of the best test minus that of the
  test chosen under the corrupted model, same reader and layer) and the change in log loss; against
  exact KL from the truth, calibration error, and reader-weighted error |E_W|.
- Decision rule: supported if the Spearman correlation of regret with |E_W| exceeds that with KL by
  at least 0.20 with the interval of the difference excluding 0 (bootstrap over patients within
  seeds), and the matched pair's regrets differ with the interval excluding 0.
- Result that kills the claim: the margin is not met and the matched pair does not differ.

## Part D: scale repair and multi-step behavior (section 9.5)

- For every R4 family, both variants, four budgets: exact (W0's I(Y; O | h, a)); B-model of W1;
  B-model of W2-cc; B-held-out from the simulated training patients exactly as on charts: the
  mean reduction in log loss of a twin reference reader (a regularized partial-evidence logistic
  classifier fitted on simulated training prefixes in random order, cross-fitted in five folds),
  by test and by the strata of section 5.11 (results already read 0, 1, 2 or more × reference
  reader's largest probability above 0.8), taking the one-sided lower 90% bootstrap bound;
  eligible only if that bound does not exceed the mean reduction on a disjoint fold.
- Stress set: B, 0.5 B, 2 B, and B with independent lognormal noise of sigma 0.7 (corruption
  stream).
- Reported: exact expected log loss, accuracy and utility; the eligibility test's admission rate
  of overestimated budgets; at every horizon 1 to 6 on the reduced twin, the three terms of
  5.12 (iii) for raw, projected, and scaled-and-projected beliefs.
- Variant rule (section 5.11): the variant used outside the twin is the one with the lower
  worst-case exact expected log loss over the stress set, averaged over reader families under W2.
  A tie (paired 95% interval over seeds includes 0) goes to shrink-only.
- Decision rules (section 13): scale repair present if, with the exact budget under W0, the
  over-reactive, under-reactive and independence families each improve on both raw and
  projection-only with the paired 95% interval excluding 0. Usable outside the twin (gate G2c) if
  the same holds for the selected variant under W2 with the estimated conservative budget, and the
  eligibility test admits an overestimated budget in at most 10% of stress cases.
- Result that kills the claim: no improvement even with the exact budget.

## Run discipline

- Candidate sets are the grids above; nothing is selected by looking at results except through the
  track and variant rules written here.
- Maximum runs: code fixes and re-runs are allowed until E1 completes, each recorded with its
  commit; no rule or threshold in this card changes after a result is seen.
- Compute: CPU, on the Mac or on cai6734-b. GPU-hours 0.
- Dependencies: E0 passed; twin repairs verified (G1).
- Outputs: per-seed result tables in the study's synthetic results namespace, with manifests.

## Readings fixed before the first run (November 13, 2026)

Decided by the executing agent on the implementer's report, before any E1 result existed. The full
list of implementation readings is in tii-cee/tiicee/experiments/e1/README.md, committed with the
runners and the rule code (rules.py).

- R1. Gate G2c ("usable outside the twin") is applied literally: over-reactive, under-reactive and
  independence must each improve on both raw and projection-only under the selected variant. If the
  variant rule selects shrink-only, G2c cannot pass, because shrink-only cannot amplify an
  under-reactive reader; section 5.11 already names the label-tuned temperature as the only route
  for under-reactive readers in that case. Such a failure is reported as structural, not rescued.
- R2. "Scale repair present" is decided on the two-sided variant, since it asks whether budget
  matching can repair scale at all; shrink-only is evaluated and reported beside it.
- R3. Part C: regret is that of CEE-Select with CEE-Read on the stateless track at the decision
  point, so part C does not depend on part B's track choice; reader-weighted error for regret is
  the decision form of section 5.5 (committed utility in place of log Z); a history's KL,
  calibration error and |E_W| are means over its unrequested tests (maxima also stored); rows pool
  the eight readers, both acquisition rules and the seven controlled errors on the base variant;
  the matched pair is built at 0.02 nats.
- R4. Eligibility admission rate: a stress case is a (seed, test, stratum) cell times one stress
  factor applied to its held-out budget; it counts when the stressed budget exceeds the cell's exact
  mean information and still passes the eligibility test; the rate is over all cases, with the rate
  among overestimated cases reported beside it.
- R5. Every rule is decided on the base variant, on exact expected log loss at horizon 6 of the
  reduced twin, averaged over S_rand and S_ig. The other three variants are computed and reported;
  any variant on which a verdict would differ is reported with the verdict.
- R6. Under W0 with S_ig the reduced-twin quantities are identical across seeds; seed intervals under
  W0 measure variation over S_rand's order, not sampling, and are reported as such. Every verdict
  resting on a twenty-seed interval carries the coverage caveat of RESEARCH_LEDGER.md D-STATS item 2.
