# E7: public complete-record datasets

Study TII-CEE-1. Frozen November 13, 2026, before the first run. Specification sections 6.2, 6.3,
10.4, 13, 15. Kind: new intervention, measurement. GPU, public data only.

- Question: is the finding specific to our agent and our environment?
- Hypothesis: the identity's label-free prediction of the gain transfers across datasets and
  readers.

## Data

Chronic Kidney Disease and Hepatitis C (HCV) from the UCI Machine Learning Repository; Pima diabetes
from the source ACTMED cites; DDXPlus from the authors' official release. Each license is recorded
from its official page in M0 before download; a dataset whose license does not permit research use
is dropped with a note. Tabular sets: a 70/30 stratified split by seed 0; DDXPlus: its official
test split, 2,000 patients sampled by seed 0. Patients are the unit.

- Tests: each feature (tabular) or evidence question (DDXPlus) is a test. Numeric outcomes are
  binned into at most five quantile bins fitted on the training split; categorical outcomes keep
  their categories; a value missing from the record is UNAVAILABLE.
- Costs: ACTMED's per-feature costs where its paper or code publishes them, otherwise unit cost,
  recorded on this card's amendment line before the first run.

## Readers and pairings

Six readers from the panel: models 1 and 2 (smallest), 4 and 6 (middle), 8 and 9 (largest), on
rendered text, logit readout over class names. Two pairings per reader: the published one, the
reader sampling outcomes itself (W4: its distribution over the outcome options, K = 32 draws, with
the exact form where enumerable), and an empirical world model fitted on the training split (a
regularized model of each feature given the observed features, W2-style).

## Measurements per reader × dataset cell

Because records are complete, the realized gain is computed for every remaining test of every
patient: each patient follows one random order (behavior stream), and at each step up to horizon 5
(tabular) or 8 (DDXPlus) every remaining test is scored. Reported: Figure 1 coordinates; predicted
gain (primary: mean KL_inc, label-free; secondary: KL_inc plus the prior and world-model terms
estimated on training folds) and realized gain; log loss and accuracy raw and repaired; tests used
under threshold commit 0.8 and CEE-Commit; and the baselines of section 6.3 that apply here: raw
simulate-and-re-query scoring (ACTMED-style), arbitrage-style patching with reader-supplied outcome
weights (after Paleka et al.), prior-times-likelihood information gain under the empirical model
(BED-LLM-style), confidence-threshold commit, content-free calibration and uniform-weight
projection, and the labeled evidence temperature, cross-fitted. Each is named as an approximation
of the cited idea.

## Decision rule (section 13)

The accounting transfers if the rank correlation between predicted and realized gain across cells
has a 95% interval above 0 under a hierarchical bootstrap (datasets, then patients), and the sign
of the realized gain is predicted in at least 80% of the cells whose predicted-gain interval
excludes 0. Result that kills the claim: the interval includes 0, or sign agreement below 50%.

## Run discipline

Bench of at most 3 GPU-hours; one main run per reader, resumable per patient. One RTX PRO 6000 per
job. Estimate 150 GPU-hours; allowance 300. Dependencies: G2a, the E1-B track, the E1-D variant,
licenses recorded.
