# E4: clinical evidence replay

Study TII-CEE-1. Frozen November 13, 2026, before the first run. Specification sections 6.2 (R1,
W3, W4), 10.1, 10.3, 13, 14.1 (G3), 15, 20.4. Kind: new intervention. GPU, credentialed.
Evaluation provenance: cross-fitted (cohort X), validation (P, S, B).

- Question: which reading regime is the trained agent in, and does the CEE layer improve its final
  belief on fixed evidence?
- Fixed evidence: no new acquisition. A layer that never writes to the history follows a prefix of
  the logged trajectory, so arms A1 and A1s are evaluated here by replay.

## Cohorts

- P: the existing primary policy on its logged unguarded validation trajectories (240 admissions).
- S: each E8 seed policy on its own unguarded validation trajectories (240 each).
- X: the cross-fitted cohort: each of the 2,160 training-plus-validation admissions rolled out
  unguarded and replayed by the E10 fold policy that never trained on it.
- B: each E9 second-backbone policy on its own unguarded validation trajectories.
The test split is never opened.

## Reader probe (section 10.1), required per policy before any use

R1, primary form: the hypothesis agent's distribution over the four diseases at a history, by
teacher-forced scoring of the four canonical continuations. With no substitution, prompts are
byte-identical to the logged or rebuilt ones (launch check 7.4a) and the probe's most likely
hypothesis matches the stored greedy hypothesis on at least 99% of aligned steps; a lower rate is
investigated, and a policy that cannot conform is excluded with the reason recorded. A
five-parameter output calibration of the initial belief (four class offsets and one temperature)
is fit by cross-fitting and named as a component. The probe never writes to a trajectory. Scoring
substituted observations is data flow F2: it runs only if DATA_USE_CHECK.md passes F2; otherwise
E4 is BLOCKED (section 20.4).

## Design

At each step with a provided result: R1's belief before the result, after the real result, and
after K = 32 imagined results from W3 (retrieval over training-split results, excluding the
evaluated admission, only if data flow F1 passes) and from W4 (the policy's backbone writing
results on HiPerGator) in every case; both model-free probes; and, for commitment, the same for
every eligible test. Compute the audit quantities, tau_B under B-held-out, the realized gain and
its terms, and the corrected belief along the trajectory. Every fitted piece is cross-fitted by
admission. K sensitivity at 8 and 16 by subsampling the same draws.

## Arms (by replay)

| Arm | Acquisition | Reader and layer | Commit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Raw | The logged policy's own | R1, no layer | The policy's own stopping and final answer |
| V1 | The logged policy's own | Stored hypotheses | Hypothesis fallback, each policy's own V1 |
| A1 | The logged policy's own (a prefix) | R1 + CEE-Read on the E1-B track | CEE-Commit with the recall-aligned utility, lambda 1 |
| A1s | Same | R1 + CEE-Read + CEE-Scale (E1-D variant, B-held-out), only if G2c passed | CEE-Commit |

## Endpoints

- Primary: mean change in log loss of the final belief, A1 against raw, on cohort X; 95% paired
  bootstrap, 10,000 resamples of admissions within folds.
- Secondary: the same on P, S and B; T5 and provided-test cost of A1 and A1s against each policy's
  own V1 on identical evidence; the forced-continuation curve (error against number of results
  read, raw and corrected); the Figure 1 coordinates of every policy.

## Decision rules

- Gate G3: the primary interval excludes 0 in the improving direction, A1's T5 is not below V1's in
  point estimate on cohort X, and the repairs of sections 8.3 and 8.5 are verified.
- Layer rule for everything downstream: scale-and-projection if G2c passed and the scale stage's
  cross-fitted realized gain on cohort X has a 95% interval above 0; otherwise projection only. No
  T5 comparison enters this choice.
- A4 trigger for E5: at least 20% harmful applications in this replay.
- Result that kills the claim: the primary interval excludes 0 in the harmful direction, or no
  measurable violation.

| Component | Declaration |
|---|---|

| Acquisition | The logged policy's (fixed evidence) |

| Information access | The logged history; imagined results are never written into it |
| Policy identity | Recipe, backbone, seed, training folds and checkpoint digest per policy |
| Pairing and provenance | Admission; digests of every fitted artifact, the retrieval index and the policy checkpoint in the manifest |

## Run discipline

- Bench of at most 3 GPU-hours first. Each cohort's replay runs once; interruptions resume from the
  last completed admission under the same manifest.
- GPU: one RTX PRO 6000 per job, all E4 jobs on that type.
- Estimate 180 GPU-hours; allowance 400.
- Dependencies: G2a; probe conformance per policy; DATA_USE_CHECK.md (F2 required, F1 for W3);
  the preflight and manifest binding of section 8.3; cohorts S, X, B need E8, E10, E9.
