# E10: five cross-fitted fold policies and cohort X

Study TII-CEE-1. Frozen November 13, 2026, before the first run. Specification sections 10.3,
11.4, 13, 14.3, 15; AUTHORIZATION.md section 6 (training on the 2,160 training-plus-validation
admissions is permitted for these fold policies only). Kind: new training under the frozen recipe.
GPU, credentialed.

- Question: can clinical effects be estimated with adequate precision? Cohort X raises the clinical
  sample from 240 validation admissions to 2,160 held-out admissions.
- Provenance label for cohort X: recipe tuned on the original validation split, policies
  cross-fitted.

## Folds

- The union of the replication's prepared train.csv (1,920) and val.csv (240) admissions, 2,160 in
  all, split into five folds stratified by diagnosis with a fixed seed (20261113, folds stream).
  Each admission is identified by its patient_id and a content fingerprint; the fold table stays
  private. The test split is never copied, read or used.
- Fold policy k trains on the other four folds. The validation split's role in the recipe is
  monitoring only (launch check 7.4e: no early stopping, no checkpoint selection, final checkpoint),
  so a fixed stratified tenth of those four folds plays it: monitored, not trained on. Each fold
  policy therefore trains on about 1,555 admissions instead of 1,920, a recorded deviation in data
  volume that the cross-fitting requires.
- Recipe: E8's frozen primary recipe with seed 269; two epochs over the fold's training portion.

## Runs

- Five trainings, split into parts of at most 24 hours with forced checkpoints at Slurm's warning.
- Each fold policy rolls out unguarded on its own held-out fold (432 admissions each), with the full
  prompt and its digest logged privately at every step; its V0 and V1 per held-out admission.
- Probe conformance per fold policy before any E4 use.

## Decision rule

Integrity only, checked by fingerprints: every admission of cohort X is scored by exactly one fold
policy, and that policy's training and monitoring sets do not contain it. Any leakage between a fold
policy and its held-out admissions kills cohort X.

## Run discipline

- One RTX PRO 6000 per job; accounts cai6734 then cai5724.
- Estimate 133 GPU-hours (25.6 per training scaled by data volume, 0.458 per 240 rollouts);
  allowance 270.
- Dependencies: section 8.3 preflight; section 8.4 tests, including "a fold policy never scores an
  admission it trained on".
