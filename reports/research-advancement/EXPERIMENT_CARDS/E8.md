## The recipe, frozen as the replication ran it (launch check 7.4e)

The replication's `primary` arm (replication-of-original/daniel-escalante/replication/config.py,
arm "primary"): LA-CDM with the hypothesis agent, confidence calibration, the test-cost reward and
incorrect_reward 0.0; backbone nvidia/Llama-3.1-Nemotron-Nano-8B-v1 at revision
54641c1611fcff44fa4865626462445e0a153fc7; LoRA r 8, alpha 16, dropout 0.1 on q, k, v, o, gate, up,
down; Adam at learning rate 1e-5, constant schedule; 2 epochs; beta 0.04; loss dr_grpo; 8 rollouts
per patient; microbatch 2; completion limit 256; max_length 4096; max_steps 13; bf16; loss
schedule 100 da / 100 ha_sft / 100 ha_conf_cal repeating; 3,840 optimizer steps on the 1,920
training admissions. The validation split is monitored only: no early stopping and no checkpoint
selection; the final fixed-budget checkpoint is the policy. No hyperparameter search.

## Runs

- Seeds 270, 271, 272, 273 (the existing policy is seed 269), each threaded through
  `config.compose(seed=)`, `transformers.set_seed` and the per-request vLLM seed streams exactly as
  the replication does.
- Data: the replication's prepared train.csv and val.csv with their existing Patient History
  Summary column, copied into the study's private directory; test.csv is never copied or read.
- Each training is split into parts of at most 24 hours: the stage catches Slurm's ten-minute
  warning, forces a checkpoint at the next step end (with the environment RNG sidecar the
  replication writes), and exits with the continue code; the next part resumes through the
  replication's own `get_last_checkpoint` path. Checkpoints every 100 steps, last five kept.
- After training, each seed policy gets its own unguarded validation rollout (V0), logged with the
  full prompt and its digest at every step (privately), and its V1 reference computed by tim's
  `v1_rows` rule.
- Probe conformance (section 10.1) per policy, before any E4 use.

## Measurements

Training completion; final checkpoint digest; validation T5 of V0 and V1 per seed; then, through
E4 and E6, the between-policy spread of the audit and replay quantities.

## Decision rule

Descriptive. A seed whose training fails after the stage's bounded retries is reported as failed
and the others proceed; nothing is retuned.
