# E3: structured readers on charts

Study TII-CEE-1. Frozen November 13, 2026, before the first run. Specification sections 6.2 (R2,
R3, W1, W2, W2-cc), 8.2, 9.6, 13, 15. Kind: new intervention, measurement. CPU, credentialed,
aggregates only. Evaluation provenance: cross-fitted.

- Question: do structured readers on charts show the same accounting as the twin?
- Data: MIMIC-IV-Ext CDM v1.1 in approved private storage. Fitting reads the training split;
  scoring reads the validation split; the test split is never opened. Lab content only, the scope
  of the content model of PR #9.

## Readers and world models

- R2: the structured content reader of PR #9 (tim/content.py at acf5254), as stored (absent items
  carry no evidence, likelihood tau 13) and untempered (tau 1). Reached through an adapter; PR #9
  is not edited.
- R3: a regularized multinomial logistic partial-evidence classifier on training-split prefixes in
  randomized order, with the same evidence representation as R2 (the HPI prior's features and the
  observed lab items), so R2 and R3 differ in the reader and not in the evidence. Regularization
  chosen by five-fold cross-validation on the training split only; output calibrated by
  cross-fitting.
- W1: PR #9's class-conditional content model. W2: per-item regularized multinomial regression on
  history features and already observed bins, respecting evidence identity; W2-cc its
  class-conditional form for B-model. Regularization chosen on the training split only.
- Scoring (REVIEW-001 repair, required first): learned and reference models score identical item
  targets with identical inclusion, conditioning, smoothing and weights; the reference is fitted
  separately from pooled training outcomes under the matching chart-availability condition; joint
  panel likelihood is a separately named metric.

## Design

- The validation admissions, five folds stratified by class, and the existing eight chart orders.
  Every fitted piece that touches validation labels (output calibration, budgets, gate bounds) is
  fitted on the folds that exclude the scored admission.
- At each decision point: the audit quantities; tau_B under B-held-out and under B-model; the
  realized gain and its three estimated terms; posterior log loss and Brier score raw, projected,
  and scaled-and-projected (the scale stage only if gate G2c passes, otherwise reported as not
  applied); budget calibration by stratum; the mass of negative raw scores before and after
  projection.
- Intervals: 95% paired percentile bootstrap, 10,000 resamples of admissions; the eight orders and
  all prefixes of one admission stay together.

## Decision rule

Benefit if the 95% interval of the change in posterior log loss for R2-stored, projected against
raw, excludes 0 in the improving direction; otherwise report the identity's terms. Not a
kill-type experiment: it is a measurement.
