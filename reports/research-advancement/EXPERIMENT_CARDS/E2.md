# E2: the language-model reader panel on twin text

Study TII-CEE-1. Frozen November 13, 2026, before the first run. Specification sections 6.2 (R5,
W4), 10.2, 13, 14.3. Kind: new intervention, measurement. GPU, no patient data. Evaluation
provenance: exact (twin) for the references; the readers are zero-shot.

- Question: which regime are language-model readers in, and does each repair help them?
- Hypotheses, one per regime of section 10.2; more than one may hold for a reader.

## Design

- Readers R5: the nine models of AUTHORIZATION.md section 5 at the pinned revisions, zero-shot,
  bf16, greedy scoring with no sampling. Primary readout: logit scoring of the four class-name
  continuations, normalized over the four classes. Secondary readout, verbalized probabilities, on
  models 4 (OLMo-2-1124-7B-Instruct) and 9 (OLMo-2-0325-32B-Instruct), one family at two sizes.
- Patients: 1,000 patients of the full twin, base variant, seed 0, evaluation split, generation
  stream; three renderings each (the twin's three templates).
- Selectors: random order (behavior stream) and greedy exact information gain under W0. Horizon 8.
- At every step: the reader's belief now, and its belief after each of the five findings and
  UNAVAILABLE for every remaining test (exact enumeration of the outcome set; no sampling).
- World models: W0 (the truth), and W4, the reader as its own sampler: the reader's distribution
  over the six outcome options for the requested test, by logit scoring of the outcome
  continuations; this is the published simulate-and-re-query recipe. The error of K = 4, 8, 16, 32
  draws from that distribution is reported against its exact form.
- Layers: none; CEE-Read on the track chosen in E1-B; CEE-Scale in the variant chosen in E1-D with
  the exact budget of W0. Both are computed offline from the stored reader outputs; no extra
  reader calls.

## Measurements per reader (Figure 1 coordinates with intervals)

tau_B under the exact budget (two-sided diagnostic) and location incoherence at tau_B;
responsiveness; the two model-free probes (reaction to UNAVAILABLE, which is uninformative in the
base variant, and to exact re-exposure of an already observed result); exact change in expected
log loss under projection alone and under the selected scale variant, on both tracks; the share
of regret by component from one-step quantities and paired counterfactual replays; sensitivity to
rendering and to the order of history items in the prompt; the K-sample error.

Intervals: 95% percentile bootstrap, 10,000 resamples, resampling patients with their three
renderings kept together.

## Decision rule (section 10.2, fixed)

Scale regime if the 95% interval of log tau_B excludes 0. Drift regime if the interval of location
incoherence at tau_B lies above the same quantity for R0 under identical sampling. Unresponsive if
responsiveness is below 5% of the exact budget. Repairs are judged by the E1 rules. Result that
kills the claim: every reader indistinguishable from R0, so there is nothing to audit.

## Section 6.1 contract

| Component | Declaration |
|---|---|
| World model | W0 exact; W4 reader-as-sampler, exact over six options; K error reported |
| Reader | R5 models 1 to 9, rendered twin text, logit readout over class names; no output calibration |
| Coherence layer | None; CEE-Read on the E1-B track |
| Scale layer | None; CEE-Scale, E1-D variant, exact budget |
| Gate | None (reported ungated; gate counts reported as in E1) |
| Acquisition | Random order; greedy exact IG under W0 |
| Commit | Step limit 8; threshold 0.8; CEE-Commit, computed offline |
| Availability | Population availability of the twin |
| Information access | Ordinary: rendered history. W0 is a declared reference |
| Policy identity | Not applicable; model identity and revision per reader |
| Pairing | Identical patients, renderings and selector paths for every reader |

## Run discipline

- Bench first, at most 3 GPU-hours, which replaces the estimate. Then one main run per reader,
  split into resumable parts of at most 24 hours, checkpointed per patient by fingerprint.
- GPU: one RTX PRO 6000 per job (gpu:rtx_pro_6000:1); every job of E2 on that type.
- Estimate 90 GPU-hours; allowance 200 (AUTHORIZATION.md section 4).
- Dependencies: gate G2a (E0 passed, E1 complete), the E1-B track, the E1-D variant; the models
  downloaded at their pinned revisions (a gated or relicensed model is a BLOCKED item and the rest
  of the panel proceeds).
